# Privacy Policy: Phulka

*Last updated: 27 September 2026*

Phulka ("the app") is made by Harkirat Singh.

**We do not collect any personal data.** The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

**What stays on your device.** Your game progress (the day you are on, your coins, upgrades bought and your settings) is saved in a small file on your own device so you can continue where you left off. It never leaves your device, except through your phone's own backup (for example Google or iCloud backup), which is controlled by you and your phone's settings. Deleting the app deletes this file.

**Permissions.** The app asks for no special permissions.

**Children.** The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this ever changes (for example, if an online feature is added), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
